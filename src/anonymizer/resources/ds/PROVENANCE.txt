packaging/app-icon.ico is the original favicon.ico; app-icon.png is the largest
48px frame resampled to 256px with Pillow LANCZOS. See brand-provenance.json.

Active palette: primary #4769ff, dark chrome/text #04143a, canvas #f4f6fa,
white cards and fields. Desktop secondary text uses dark neutral #04143a
instead of the web overlay's near-white content-secondary on a light surface.
Primary hover #3d5ce1 and pressed #3350c4 are explicit desktop adaptations:
85/15 and 70/30 RGB mixes of primary/dark, rounded to nearest byte.
Status palette comes from brand-palette.ts (norm, warning, problem, accent, neutral).
Neutral borders/disabled-button text, dimensions and corners inherit the base DS.

The older colors/typography/spacing/base/fonts.css and logo-in.png remain the
unchanged foundational snapshot from C:/Git/scg/In.Plan Design System.zip.
Their purple/Open Sans values are historical source data, not active GUI colors.
The new brand supersedes the earlier product/presentation style distinction.

Primary fonts are Manrope 400/600/700, decoded and merged from the supplied
Latin/Cyrillic WOFF2 subsets with app-private InPlan Desktop Manrope GDI aliases.
Private aliases prevent collisions with installed variable Manrope fonts. See fonts/manrope-provenance.json.
Original Open Sans files and license remain the bundled fallback.
Generate resources with tools/prepare_brand.py in the pinned build-only environment;
do not hand-edit binary resources or generated manifests.
